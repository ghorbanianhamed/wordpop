Privacy

WordPop is a small learning project. Here is exactly what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your email and a hashed password – to sign you in. Your password is never stored in readable form.
  • Your words, categories and settings – the content you add.
  • Your quiz answers and when you last used the app – to schedule reviews, show your progress, and see (in total, not per person) whether the app is useful.
  • A push notification address for your device – only if you turn notifications on.
  • Your WordPop Plus status – if you subscribe: your plan, renewal date and a Stripe customer number. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach WordPop.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no selling or sharing your data.
  • No tracking across other websites.

Third parties

When you use “Look up”, the word (and nothing else) is sent to the free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev) and, if needed, to Wiktionary. Notifications are delivered through Apple's, Google's or Mozilla's push services. Password-reset emails are sent through our email provider. With the natural voices, the text being read aloud (and nothing else) is sent to Microsoft Azure AI Speech to make the recording. Payments for WordPop Plus are processed by Stripe, which receives your email address and payment details.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete my account (step-by-step). Everything linked to your account is removed immediately, and any WordPop Plus subscription is cancelled.
