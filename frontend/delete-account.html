Delete your WordPop account

You can delete your account and all of its data yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

In the app (Android, iPhone or web)

  1. Open WordPop and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom right).
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account and all data, then confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password)
  • All your entries, categories, settings, quiz answers and progress
  • Your notification addresses and any stored voice recordings linked to you
  • Any WordPop Plus subscription is cancelled, so you won't be charged again

Everything is removed immediately and can't be recovered. Payment records that the law requires us to keep for accounting are held by our payment provider, Stripe.

Can't sign in?

If you can't sign in, first use Forgot password? to get back into your account and delete it as above. If that doesn't work, write to the support address on your Stripe receipt, or reply to any WordPop email, from the email address of your account, and ask for it to be deleted. We will delete it within 30 days.
